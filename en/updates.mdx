---
title: "Update SpendLess"
description: "Install a newer version and find out what changed."
---

SpendLess notifies you when a newer version is available. You can also compare your installed version in **Settings → About** with the [website changelog](https://spendlessdaw.app/changelog).

<Tabs>
<Tab title="macOS">
Updates are installed **manually** while the app is not Developer ID signed and notarized.

1. Choose **Download update**, or download the latest macOS installer from the website.
2. Save your work and quit SpendLess.
3. Open the new **.dmg**.
4. Drag SpendLess into **Applications** and replace the previous application.
5. Reopen SpendLess and check the version.

If first-launch security appears again, follow [the installation guide](/en/installation). Check Accessibility permission if project detection stops working.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Windows">
Windows supports automatic app updates. Follow the in-app update prompts and save your work before restarting. If the update fails, download the current Windows installer from the [official website](https://spendlessdaw.app/#download).
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Before a major change
Keep a recent [backup](/en/backups), especially before moving computers or reinstalling. Replacing the app is different from deleting your projects or resetting your app data.
