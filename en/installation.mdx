---
title: "Install SpendLess"
description: "Download and open SpendLess on Windows or macOS, including first-launch security messages."
---

Download your installer from the [official SpendLess website](https://spendlessdaw.app/#download). You also need **FL Studio installed** to open and edit your music projects.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="macOS">
The current macOS release requires **macOS 13 or later**. The universal download works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

<Steps>
<Step title="Install the app">Download the **.dmg**, open it, and drag **SpendLess** into **Applications**.</Step>
<Step title="Open SpendLess">Launch it from Applications. SpendLess is not yet Developer ID signed or notarized, so macOS may block this first launch.</Step>
<Step title="Allow your official download">If macOS says it cannot verify the developer or check the app, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Find the SpendLess message and choose **Open Anyway**, if available, then confirm. Only do this for the official download you intended to install.</Step>
<Step title="Allow project detection">In **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**, enable **SpendLess**. This allows the app to read FL Studio window titles and recognize your open project. Restart SpendLess if detection does not update.</Step>
</Steps>

For the current macOS procedure, see [Apple's guide to downloaded apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445). A message about malware or a damaged app is different: stop and [contact support](/en/troubleshooting#ask-for-help).
</Tab>
<Tab title="Windows">
<Steps>
<Step title="Download the installer">Choose **Download for Windows** on the website and open the downloaded installer.</Step>
<Step title="Review SmartScreen">SpendLess is not yet signed with a trusted publisher certificate, so Windows may show **Windows protected your PC**. For your official download, choose **More info**, check the app, then **Run anyway** if offered.</Step>
<Step title="Complete installation">Follow the installer, then open SpendLess and finish the first-launch setup.</Step>
</Steps>

If **Run anyway** is unavailable on a managed computer, ask its administrator. Do not disable Windows security to install the app. [Microsoft explains SmartScreen reputation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/package-and-deploy/smartscreen-reputation).
</Tab>
</Tabs>
