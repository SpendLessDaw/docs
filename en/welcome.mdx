## A simple session
Open a project from SpendLess, make music in FL Studio, then **save and close the project**. SpendLess can refresh the saved information and collect the samples it can find. Attach a fresh audio render when you want a new preview.

SpendLess organizes your work; FL Studio remains where you compose, mix and render audio. Plugins and their licenses stay installed separately.
